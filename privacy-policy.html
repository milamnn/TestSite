1. Scope

TAREEQ AL MOSTAQBAL TRADING AND RENTING VENDING MACHINES L.L.C. (“TEMBOT”, “we”, “us” or “our”) processes personal data in connection with the TEMBOT website, enquiries, demonstrations, business communications and related services in the United Arab Emirates.

We are committed to responsible handling of personal data in accordance with applicable UAE requirements, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the “UAE PDPL”), to the extent applicable to our processing activities.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with TEMBOT, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, business email, phone number, company, job title and professional contact details.
  • Enquiry information: information supplied when requesting a demo, discussing deployment or contacting us.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser/device information, pages viewed, timestamps and similar information where collected by website technologies.
  • Communications: correspondence and information contained in emails or other communications.

We generally do not need sensitive personal data for ordinary website enquiries. Please do not submit sensitive personal data through the website unless we specifically request it through an appropriate channel.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Respond to enquiries and demo requests.
  • Communicate with prospective and existing business customers.
  • Provide, maintain and improve our website and services.
  • Understand website usage and improve user experience.
  • Manage business relationships and prospective deployments.
  • Protect our systems, facilities and business against misuse, fraud and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory or governmental requirements.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable law, we process personal data on an appropriate legal basis. Depending on the circumstances, this may include your consent, performance or preparation of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of legitimate interests, or another basis permitted under applicable UAE data protection law.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent, subject to applicable legal requirements and any consequences explained when consent is requested.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose personal data where reasonably necessary to operate our business, including to service providers supporting hosting, IT, communications, analytics, security and other business functions; professional advisers; auditors and legal counsel; competent authorities or regulators where required or permitted by law; and relevant parties in a corporate transaction.

Relevant service providers are expected to handle personal data only for authorised purposes and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

6. International Transfers

Some service providers or business partners may process personal data outside the United Arab Emirates. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by applicable UAE law to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including business records, contractual or legal obligations, dispute resolution and enforcement of agreements.

When data is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it in accordance with applicable law and our retention practices.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse or destruction.

No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe your information has been compromised.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable UAE law and relevant exemptions, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights to:

  • obtain information about how your data is processed;
  • request access to personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion or restriction of processing where provided by law;
  • object to certain processing activities;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • exercise any applicable right to data portability or other rights provided by UAE data protection law.

To exercise a right, contact us using Section 13. We may need to verify your identity. Requests will be handled within the period required by applicable law.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for operation, security, preferences, analytics or functionality.

Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies. You can also control cookies through your browser settings. If TEMBOT introduces analytics, advertising, embedded media or other third-party technologies, this policy or a separate cookie notice may be updated accordingly.

11. Children

Our website and business services are intended for business and professional audiences and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data through the website.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements or privacy practices. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, providing additional notice.
